# Lebanon Under Pressure 

Lebanon Under Pressure is an interactive Streamlit data-visualization project built on the same Lebanon dataset from my Plotly assignment. I'm presenting the data as an archival editorial journal, letting my readers explore through the country's recent history rather than just read a chart. 

We have two archives: 

Archive 01- War & Life Expectancy: how life expectancy bends around selected conflict years. 

Archive 02: How female unemployment and the refugee population moved through the 2019-2021 crisis. 

## The interactions

The reader chooses a conflict, then reveal the conflict year and its aftermath; or depending on the archive issue, they can choose a crisis year and trace the pressure.
